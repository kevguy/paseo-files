# Third-party source

PDF/Office viewer code under `client/doc-viewer`, `server/doc-viewer` and `shared/doc-viewer` is from nkwib/paseo-doc-viewer, MIT, commit `5b7cee29d7e9d57505b9e435bdf7af0a78a126ff` (v0.8.0). Source: https://github.com/nkwib/paseo-doc-viewer . Its LICENSE, NOTICE and local change log are retained in `vendor/doc-viewer`. Other libraries retain their licenses in their distributions.

Desktop/web editing uses CodeMirror (MIT) and @replit/codemirror-vim (MIT): https://codemirror.net/ and https://github.com/replit/codemirror-vim . This is Vim emulation in an editor, not a shell or an embedded Neovim process.
